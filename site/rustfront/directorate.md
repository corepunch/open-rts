# Directorate

The Directorate combines conventional armour with transport, repair, and long-range missiles. Scout with a buggy, move infantry in an APC, and keep expensive armour near a Field Crane.

## Tech and battlefield roles

- The **Command Center** builds structures. The **Power Plant** opens the **Ore Refinery** and **Barracks**. The refinery supplies a starting **Ore Harvester** and opens the **War Factory**. The Barracks opens the **Gun Turret** and fills the frontline with **Riflemen** and **Rocket Soldiers**.
- The **Scout Buggy** spots and counters light targets. The **Battle Tank** is general-purpose armour. **Artillery** supports the line with long-range shells. A Barracks adds access to the **Mastodon**, which fires two heavy shells per volley.
- The **Uplink Centre** unlocks the **Hammer** and **Kestrel**. The Hammer fires two splashing missiles to 11 cells, with a 2.5-cell minimum range and 6-cell sight. The Kestrel flies over obstacles and attacks ground targets with paired rockets.
- The **APC** carries up to five infantry. **Load** sends selected soldiers to an APC with room. **Unload** releases its passengers around it. A destroyed APC ejects its passengers at half strength.
- The **Field Crane** is unarmed and repairs the most damaged friendly vehicle or structure within two cells, at 30 HP per second for vehicles or 60 for structures.
- The **Phantom** phases for three seconds when hit with its ability ready. While phased it cannot be targeted, damaged, or fire. The phase ability has a 20-second recharge.
- **Deploy** converts the **Sentry Truck** into a **Sentry Post**, an anti-air machine-gun emplacement and detector. **Pack Up** converts it back to a truck.
