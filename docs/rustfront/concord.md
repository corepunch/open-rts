# Lumen Concord

The Concord fields expensive units with long-range beams and overlapping support. Keep the heavy weapons behind a screen and bring an Aegis to a sustained engagement.

## Tech and battlefield roles

- The **Spire** builds structures and deals Rites. The **Fusion Core** opens the **Extractor** and **Academy**. The Extractor brings a **Hover Harvester** and opens the **Foundry**. The Academy trains infantry, including the **Warden** and **Lancer**, and unlocks the **Beam Tower**.
- The **Skimmer** scouts and fires on aircraft. The **Glaive** is the main armoured line. The **Prism** fires a heavy splashing beam at ground targets, while the **Strider** arcs from its target to one nearby enemy.
- The **Sanctum** unlocks the flying **Seraph** and the **Aegis**. The Seraph beams ground and air targets. The Aegis reduces incoming damage by one third for friendly forces in a 3.5-cell radius and repairs friendly vehicles by 12 HP per second.
- The unarmed **Cantor** detects hidden objects and heals the three most injured friendly infantry within three cells, by 10 HP each per second.
- **Salvage** pays 10% of the production cost for qualifying enemy machines and structures the Concord destroys. Infantry yield no salvage.

## Rites

A Spire deals a card every 105 seconds into a hand that holds up to three. Play cards from the command bar. Targeted cards show the affected radius before you click.

| Rite | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Strike | Lands after two seconds and deals 150 damage to enemies within 1.25 cells |
| Mending | Friendly units within three cells recover one third of maximum HP. Structures recover one fifth. |
| Windfall | Adds 200 credits |
| Warding | Friendly units within three cells take half damage for five seconds |
