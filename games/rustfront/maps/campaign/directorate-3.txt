title The Ford
briefing The Glass River cuts the Rustfront in two, deep in its canyon. Only two fords cross it, and the Clans have dug in at the southern one with a War Camp and spike towers.
briefing Your War Factory is cleared to build Scout Buggies, Battle Tanks and APCs. Take the southern ford and hold it for a minute so the engineers can mark it, then burn out the War Camp on the far bank.
briefing Ramps lead down the canyon walls at each ford; the walls themselves cannot be climbed.
