**  The Story:  **
The Imperium’s Dessicator weapon was complete, and Ruling 
Directorate decided to use it to put a conclusive end to the 
conflict. The Imperium’s informant in commander Radec’s 
command, Stiv Baator, informed the Imperium of the location 
of the primary Freedom Guard stronghold on Strata. Radec 
learned that Baator was a mole and had him executed, but not 
before Strata’s location was compromised. While the Imperium 
fleet orbited Strata with the Dessicator, ground troops assaulted 
the heavily defended Freedom Guard base in an attempt to 
disable the Freedom Guard’s orbital defense matrix so that the 
Dessicator could reach the planet. 
.
.
**  Briefing:  **
Commander Radec has discovered a mole in his command. The
agent has been executed, but not before he revealed the
location of our primary headquarters on Strata. The Imperium
is orbiting the planet, presumably with the Dessicator, and
large ground force is moving toward the power core for the
matrix. If it falls, nothing will prevent them from destroying
Strata. Eliminate the Imperium presence and do not allow the
power core to be destroyed.
