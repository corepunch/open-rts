**  The Story:  **
The Imperium’s Dessicator weapon was complete, and Ruling 
Directorate decided to use it to put a conclusive end to the 
conflict. The Imperium’s informant in commander Radec’s 
command, Stiv Baator, informed the Imperium of the location 
of the primary Freedom Guard stronghold on Strata. Radec 
learned that Baator was a mole and had him executed, but not 
before Strata’s location was compromised. While the 
Imperium fleet orbited Strata with the Dessicator, ground 
troops assaulted the heavily defended Freedom Guard base in 
an attempt to disable the Freedom Guard’s orbital defense 
matrix so that the Dessicator could reach the planet. 
.
.
**  Briefing:  **
The power core for the orbital defense matrix must be
destroyed. General Trilkin himself is in orbit above the
planet waiting to implement the Dessicator. Move into the
base and eliminate the power core. When you have succeeded,
all ground forces will be evacuated and the planet can be
eliminated.
.
