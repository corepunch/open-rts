**  The Story:  **
The Imperium’s hover technology proved to be a considerable 
tactical advantage in most ground engagements. The ability of 
Imperium armor to move over water allowed them to dictate 
the circumstances of conflicts and made it difficult for even the 
most capable Freedom Guard commanders to marshal decisive 
victories. The Freedom Guard felt they must obtain this 
technology in order to challenge the Imperium’s dominion. 
The Freedom Guard discovered an Imperium research base on 
the planet Malik where advances to the hover drive were being 
developed and assaulted the base in an attempt to steal the 
vital technology.
.
.
**  Briefing:  **
The security of the research site appears to have been
compromised. A sizable Freedom Guard contingent has
established a makeshift base on the mainland coast, and an
infiltrator has stolen vital information that may allow the
Freedom Guard to duplicate our hover technology. Assault the
Freedom Guard position and be sure to eliminate all traces
of the enemy. We may be able to destroy the data before
they can move it off world.
.
                        OBJECTIVES
