**  The Story:  **
As the Freedom Guard won its first minor victories and 
demonstrated that they could challenge the Imperium, popular 
support for the rebellion spread quickly. Initially, the Freedom 
Guard armed forces were composed entirely of individuals 
from the Marked caste, but growing numbers of the common 
citizenry began to sympathize with the Freedom Guard cause 
and join in the struggle. Imperium troops attacked civilian 
populations across the planet Teron, where the first armed 
resistance had occurred centuries earlier, in an attempt to 
discourage support for the insurgents.
