**  The Story:  **
The Imperium’s hover technology proved to be a 
considerable tactical advantage in most ground 
engagements. The ability of Imperium armor to move over 
water allowed them to dictate the circumstances of conflicts 
and made it difficult for even the most capable Freedom 
Guard commanders to marshal decisive victories. The 
Freedom Guard felt they must obtain this technology in 
order to challenge the Imperium’s dominion. The Freedom 
Guard discovered an Imperium research base on the planet 
Malik where advances to the hover drive were being 
developed and assaulted the base in an attempt to steal the 
vital technology.
.
.
**   Briefing:  **
The research facility is not heavily guarded, as its location
is believed to be unknown. Establish an attack base on the
island and assault the Imperium position. Steal the plans for 
any of the hover vehicles so that we can attempt to develop 
a hover driven weapon of our own. Then level the complex.
.
                           OBJECTIVES
- Steal the plans for any of the Imperium hover vehicles.
- Destroy all Imperium structures.
