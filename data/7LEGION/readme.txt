                     7th Legion
                     Version 1.0

                     Readme File
                   1 September 1997
 ===========================================================

 IMPORTANT: Please read this text file for help with installing and
 running 7th Legion and for last minute changes that may have not
 made it into the manual.

 Game Play:
 ==========
 1) The user will only receive kill credits toward special mission
 requirements and rank if he destroys the target by gun/ray/missile
 fire.  Tank overruns, cards and kills by gun emplacements do not
 count.

 2) On some infrequent occasions, you will select a unit and the
 mouse cursor will not change to the "movement/target" pointer.
 To undo this effect, just click and drag a box around the unit
 in question, just like you were selecting a large group of units.
 The mouse pointer will then regain normal function.

 3) The colors for the following battle-cards are incorrect:
         Doom Fist 2 (should be red, because man, it HURTS!)
         Joker       (should be blue, as it can sometimes
                     deal out a harmful card, and sometimes
                     a beneficial one).

 4) In multiplayer only, the Doom Fist card causes damage to
 ALL walls in the game.  (Even yours!)

 5) The Balance card distributes credits evenly to all human and
 AI players.

 6) When you have built the maximum number of units allowable, you
 can try to build more, and the game will go through the motions of
 building them; but no new units will be added and no money will be 
 takenfrom your budget.

 7) If you demolish a building using the "Destruct" button, you
 must click on another building in order to create any new units.
 Clicking on the Build Panel will not select buildings after a
 demolition.

 8) The manual section "Structure Damage and Loss" refers to a
 status meter.  All units and buildings have this.  It is the bar
 which appears beneath a building or unit when you select it.
 Lots of yellow on the bar is good.  It indicates how much health
 you have left.  When it goes all black, the health is gone...

 9) The lightning zapper weapon of the Purifier tank can sometimes
 cause the screen to flicker.  There should be no other ill effects
 (except of course to the targets of the tank!)

 10) Units may sometimes seem to be walking off into the sunset.
 What is actually happening is that the path to the desired
 destination was blocked, either permanently by terrain or temporarily
 by another unit, and the unit is desperately trying to find a way to
 obey your orders.

 11) The map graphics are an abstract interpretation of real terrain.
 There are several grid squares on the map that might look partially
 accessible to units, but are, in fact, obstructed.   If you are
 trying to get a unit to move to a certain spot, and it won't do so,
 this is why.

 12) You can deploy units as they are built by clicking on one of
 the numbers in the unit build window, holding down the left mouse
 button and dragging it onto the map.  Release the button at the point
 where you want your newly built units to go.  Usually, the mouse
 pointer will change to the deploy cursor when you do this.
 Occasionally, however, the deploy cursor will remain behind and you
 will see the regular pointer cursor as you carry out this procedure.
 The units being built will still go to the area you select.

 13) In interior missions, you must move your marines yourself to
 positions where they can fire at an enemy unit.  The computer AI will
 not move them for you.  This is so you can have finer control over
 their movements, as the interior missions contain many ambush
 possibilities.

 14)  Units firing at each other due to the Chaos effect will not stop
 firing after you play a change effect card, such as Skill Strip,  on
 the chaotic units.  To stop them from firing at each other, you will
 have to select the units and give them different orders.


 Drive Space Issues:
 ===================
 The game on compact install requires 15MB of hard drive space.  But
 DirectX5 (If you choose to install it) will require an additional 15MB.

 If you're running 7th Legion near the minimum system specifications
 (15mb hard drive space and 16mb ram), we recommend that you save 
 after every two or three missions, then exit and restart 7th Legion.


 Manual Errata
 =============
 1) The Autoplay feature will not engage if the game is already
 installed on your machine.  Autoplay will only run the installation,
 not launch the game.

 2) The "Deploy" text will appear over the mobile base unit even if
 it is in an area in which it cannot in fact be deployed.  If you
 try to deploy and nothing happens, there wasn't enough room.


 Multiplayer Issues:
 ===================
 System Requirements:
 Minimum CPU speed for multiplayer is an IBM PC Pentium 133MHz. We
 highly recommend that the fastest machine be the host for 7th Legion
 multiplay. 

 1) In multiplayer, the color of the buildings in the "build bar" will
 always appear blue if you are 7th Legion and Red if you are Chosen
 no matter what color you are in the game.

 2) When the host leaves the "Setup Screen," all players connected to
 the host will be dropped to the "Menu Screen".

 3) If you are using modem connection, we highly recommend that you
 turn off modem compression.  If compression is on, you may experience
 problems.

 4) If you are playing Chosen in multiplay, the Venom Typhoon is labeled
 as the Revelator.

 DirectPlay 5.0:
 In order to play 7th Legion over the internet, the host player needs
 to send his IP address to the other players who wish to join into
 the game. You can find out the IP address of your machine on Windows
 95 by going to the "Start Menu," selecting Run and typing "winipcfg".
 On NT use "ipconfig".  If you have a network card AND a modem 
 installed, you will need to make sure you read the IP address of the 
 MODEM connection to the Internet. The IP address information for the 
 modem will typically show up under "PPP Adapter".

 When you are using TCP/IP connection, DirectPlay will minimize 7th
 Legion and bring up the desktop screen with an "Enter the IP Address"
 dialog box.  7th Legion will return automatically when you click on OK.

 Known limitations of Direct Play 5.0
 ====================================
 (Many of these limitations
 will be corrected in a future release of DirectPlay. Consult the
 DirectX web site http://www.microsoft.com/DirectX for updated
 information).

 - DirectPlay generally will not work through proxies and firewalls.

 - If you remove a protocol from your system after you have installed
 Direct X, the game will crash when you try to play a game with
 that protocol. If the protocol was never installed into your system
 and you select it in the game, it will just go back to the main menu.

 Hardware Compatibility and Direct X 5.0:
 ========================================
 Please check to be sure that your video card and sound card support
 Microsoft's DirectX version 5.0. If any of your hardware does not
 support DirectX version 5.0, please check with your hardware
 manufacturer to obtain the most up-to-date drivers that do support
 Microsoft's DirectX version 5.0 or later.

 You can get the latest drivers from the manufacturer's Web site
 or BBS. Please refer to your owner's manual for information on
 the manufacturer's phone numbers, Web addresses (URLs) and BBS
 numbers.

 Most of the problems with compatibility are caused by out-of-date
 drivers. These problems include choppy video, choppy sound,
 hissing noises, corrupted graphics and mouse problems.

 System Issues:
 ======================
 7th Legion may work with Win98, including the Beta versions, with a
 couple of caveats:
 1) You must have 32 mb RAM to play the game with Win98.
 2) Do not install the video and sound drivers from Direct X 5.0,
 use the drivers that come with Win98.
